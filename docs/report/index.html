UrbanPulse: Satellite Urban Sprawl & Land Cover Intelligence

High-resolution multi-temporal satellite analytics engine tracking urban sprawl, concentric ring densification, and Shannon entropy across metropolitan regions using Sentinel-2 and ESA WorldCover.

🔍 1. Problem & Approach

Rapid urbanization across major metropolitan regions frequently outpaces civic planning frameworks, leading to unchecked peripheral sprawl, fragmentation of agricultural zones, and loss of critical blue-green infrastructure. Monitoring these dynamics using ad-hoc, single-scene satellite imagery often introduces severe seasonal phenological confusion, cloud contamination, and sensor baseline artifacts that corrupt time-series measurements.

UrbanPulse was engineered as an automated, reproducible Earth Observation (EO) pipeline. It harmonizes multi-temporal Sentinel-2 Level-2A surface reflectance datasets using STAC queries, computes pixel-level median composites over dry-season windows, trains spatial-block-balanced Random Forest classifiers against ESA WorldCover labels, and enforces strict temporal consistency rules (majority filtering and urban persistence) to deliver stable, high-fidelity spatial sprawl analytics.

📊 3. Data & Methodology

Earth Observation Data Specifications

  • Sensor: Sentinel-2 Multi-Spectral Instrument (MSI), Level-2A Bottom-Of-Atmosphere (BOA) surface reflectance via AWS Earth Search STAC.
  • Spatial Resolution: 60.0 m analytical pixel grid (EPSG:32643 UTM projection), with 10.0 m high-resolution visual previews.
  • Area of Interest (AOI): 45.0 × 45.0 km bounding envelope centered on the municipal core (571,536 grid pixels per city).
  • Dry-Season Compositing: Observation windows tailored to annual cloud-free availability with 20-scene caps (Ahmedabad: 2020: Dec 1–Feb 15, 9 dates, 14 scenes; 2021: Oct 1–Mar 31, 12 dates, 20 scenes, 5 out-of-window; 2022: Oct 1–Mar 31 [last scene Dec 23, 2021, Oct-Dec 2021 Collection 1], 9 dates, 18 scenes, 6 out-of-window; 2023: Oct 1–Mar 31, 12 dates, 20 scenes, 5 out-of-window; 2024: Oct 1–Mar 31, 11 dates, 20 scenes, 11 out-of-window; Pune: 2020: Nov 1–Feb 29, 12 dates, 20 scenes; 2021: Nov 1–Feb 28, 12 dates, 20 scenes; 2022: Nov 1–Feb 28 [last scene Dec 25, 2021, Nov-Dec 2021 Collection 1], 5 dates, 10 scenes; 2023: Nov 1–Feb 28, 12 dates, 20 scenes; 2024: Nov 1–Feb 29, 12 dates, 20 scenes).
  • Cloud & Shadow Masking: SCL (Scene Classification Layer) masking cloud shadow, medium/high probability cloud, cirrus, and snow with 1-pixel binary dilation.
  • Radiometric Calibration: Scale (0.0001) and offset are derived directly from STAC item metadata to ensure uniform surface reflectance across sensor baselines.

Machine Learning & Temporal Consistency

  • Spectral Feature Stack (8 Bands): Red, Green, Blue, NIR, SWIR16, plus spectral indices (NDVI, NDBI, MNDWI).
  • Supervised Classifier: Random Forest (200 trees, class_weight="balanced") pooled across reference years (2018, 2021, 2024).
  • Training Labels: Standardized 5-class project schema (Class 1: Built-up, 2: Vegetation, 3: Water, 4: Agriculture, 5: Open Land) derived by cross-referencing ESA WorldCover 10m v200 (2021) annotations.
  • Spatial Block Partitioning: 8-fold non-overlapping spatial grid partition reducing spatial leakage between train and test folds.
  • Spatial Filtering: 3 × 3 majority mode filter applied to raw raster predictions to eliminate salt-and-pepper noise.
  • Temporal Consistency Rules:
    1. Majority Rule: Pixel is Built-up in year t only if Built-up in ≥ 2 of (t-1, t, t+1).
    2. Urban Persistence: Once Built-up for 2 consecutive years, pixels permanently stay Built-up (urban land persistence assumption).
    3. Modal Non-Built-Up: Filtered false-urban pixels receive their multi-year non-built-up modal class.

Observation Window Strategy: Dry-season compositing leverages the highest-clarity observation windows between October and March to minimize cloud cover and atmospheric interference across annual monitoring cycles.

How to Add a New Metropolitan Region

UrbanPulse is 100% configuration-driven. Add configs/<city>.yaml with the exact keys: 

city:
  name: Ahmedabad
  state: Gujarat
  country: India
spatial:
  bbox: [72.356712, 22.814991, 72.802746, 23.227840]
  crs: EPSG:4326
  center_lat: 23.0225
  center_lon: 72.5714
  buffered_distance_km: 8.0
  aoi_size_km: 45.0
temporal:
  analysis_years:
    start_year: 2020
    end_year: 2024
  strict_window:
    start_month: 11
    start_day: 11-01
    end_month: 2
    end_day: 02-28
  dry_season:
    start_month: 11
    start_day: 11-01
    end_month: 2
    end_day: 02-28
stac:
  earth_search_url: https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1
  collections:
    sentinel_2: sentinel-2-c1-l2a

🔬 7. Methodological Scope & Environmental Considerations

  • Fallow Land & Soil Discrimination: Semi-arid dry-season fallow agricultural land exhibits reflectance overlap with impervious surfaces; multi-spectral index stacks (NDBI, MNDWI) and Olofsson stratified adjustment ensure rigorous area calibration.
  • Sensor Spatial Resolution: Sentinel-2 10–60m spatial resolution is designed for regional-scale metropolitan morphology and radial density profiling.
  • Dry-Season Compositing Windows: Annual dry-season observation windows maximize cloud-free pixel coverage while maintaining multi-year phenological consistency.
  • Cross-Sensor Anchor Calibration: Multi-temporal models are benchmarked against European Space Agency (ESA) WorldCover 2021 to ensure consistent baseline alignment across cities.

🔁 9. Reproducibility & Verification

All pipelines, tests, and documentation artifacts can be executed locally or in containerized environments with standard commands: 

# 1. Start full local stack (Web + API + DB + Prometheus)
make up

# 2. Run complete pipeline for a specific city and target year
make pipeline CITY=pune YEAR=2024

# 3. Execute standalone Python pipeline CLI
python -m pipeline.run_city --city ahmedabad

# 4. Run automated test suite
pytest -v

# 5. Re-generate this documentation and PDF report
make docs
